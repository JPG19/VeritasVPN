Why VeritasVPN?

Built differently from the ground up. No compromises on privacy, transparency, or performance.

WireGuard Only

We exclusively use WireGuard — the most modern, fastest, and most auditable VPN protocol. 4,000 lines of code vs 600,000+ for legacy protocols.

No Traffic Logs

We do not log browsing history or destination traffic. We do store account, billing, and WireGuard peer metadata needed to run the service — details in our Privacy Policy.

Anonymous Accounts

No email. No name. No phone. Just a randomly generated account ID. Pay Premium with Bitcoin — no card required.

Desktop WireGuard

macOS (and Linux) desktop apps use real WireGuard tunnels. A kill switch is on the roadmap — not shipped on every platform yet.

Growing Network

Source Available (BSL)

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about VeritasVPN.

We publish source for clients, the VPN agent, and related infra under the Business Source License 1.1. We use WireGuard exclusively. Anonymous account IDs are supported, and Premium is paid with Bitcoin. Diskless RAM-only servers, multi-hop, and a large multi-region fleet are roadmap goals — not current claims.

We do not intentionally store browsing history, DNS query contents, or destination traffic metadata for later inspection. We do store account identifiers, auth/billing records, and WireGuard peer metadata required to operate tunnels. Edge/CDN access logs may exist. See the Privacy Policy for details.

WireGuard has been extensively audited, runs in the Linux kernel, and consists of only ~4,000 lines of code — making it far easier to audit and secure than OpenVPN (70,000+ lines) or IPsec (400,000+). It's also significantly faster. We believe a smaller attack surface is better for privacy. Every modern device supports WireGuard natively or via our apps.

Yes. When you create an account, you receive a randomly generated account ID — no email, name, or phone number required. This is your only credential. Save it securely, as it's the only way to recover your account. Premium is paid with Bitcoin (no card).

A warrant canary is a regularly updated statement that we have not received certain secret legal demands (for example national security letters or gag orders). Ours is published at /canary.txt. It is plain text today; cryptographic signing may come later. If it stops updating without explanation, treat that as a potential warning.

Ready today: macOS desktop (WireGuard), Linux desktop, and the Chrome extension ZIP. Windows and mobile clients are planned. Source is on GitHub under BSL 1.1.

We currently operate a single live egress region while the network expands. We will not advertise “20+ locations” or multi-provider fleets until those nodes are actually online.
